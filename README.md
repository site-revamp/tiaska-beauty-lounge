# Tiaska Beauty Lounge – Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Tiaska Beauty Lounge is a ladies' beauty salon offering haircare, skincare, nail treatments, waxing, facials, massage, Moroccan baths, makeup and other personal beauty services. The business operates across three locations in Dubai and Sharjah.

The redesigned homepage presents Tiaska as a comprehensive beauty destination, focusing on clear service discovery, accessible branch information and convenient appointment enquiries. It targets women seeking professional beauty, grooming and wellness services.

## Main Improvements

### 1. Improved Service Discovery

- Organised beauty services into clearer categories.
- Improved the presentation of haircare, skincare, nail treatments, spa services and personal grooming.
- Made the range of available treatments easier to explore.

**Problem addressed:** Visitors previously lacked a sufficiently clear way to find specific treatments, making service discovery more difficult.

### 2. Better Branch Information and Accessibility

- Improved the visibility of all three salon locations.
- Presented branch addresses and corresponding telephone contact details more clearly.
- Strengthened access to location information for customers visiting International City, Nad Al Hamar and Muweilah Commercial.

**Problem addressed:** Branch information lacked sufficient prominence, potentially making it difficult for customers to identify and contact the correct salon.

### 3. Stronger Appointment and Contact Pathways

- Improved the visibility of appointment enquiry actions.
- Prioritised direct telephone contact options.
- Created clearer opportunities for customers to enquire about treatments and arrange appointments.

**Problem addressed:** The previous website did not provide sufficiently prominent booking and contact calls to action, requiring visitors to search for ways to arrange appointments.

## Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Established a more refined beauty-focused appearance appropriate for a ladies' beauty lounge.
- **Layout:** Organised the homepage into clearer sections for services, branches and contact information.
- **User Experience:** Improved navigation between treatment categories and business locations.
- **Content Consistency:** Created a more structured presentation of the salon's services and branch information.
- **Conversion Focus:** Increased the prominence of appointment enquiries and direct contact options.

## Overall Result

The redesigned homepage gives Tiaska Beauty Lounge a clearer and more organised digital presence. It makes beauty services easier to discover, improves access to the three salon locations and provides more visible contact pathways, helping potential customers move from exploring treatments to making an appointment enquiry.
